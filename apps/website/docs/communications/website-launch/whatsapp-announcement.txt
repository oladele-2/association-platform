*NMA OGUN: A WEEK OF THANKSGIVING, A NEW CHAPTER OF CONNECTION*

Dear esteemed colleagues,

As we gathered for the thanksgiving church service marking the beginning of Physicians’ Week on Sunday, 18 October 2026, we were reminded of the fellowship and shared purpose that unite us as doctors. The accompanying photographs capture moments from that gathering.

Alongside this special beginning, we are pleased to introduce the NMA Ogun State website:

https://nmaogun.com.ng

The website gives our association a public home for official news, articles, programmes, leadership information, affiliate profiles and photographs of our community. It is a place to stay connected with the work and people of NMA Ogun, and to share our story with colleagues and the wider public.

You can now explore the Physicians’ Week programme, view photographs from the thanksgiving service, browse professional articles and learn more about our executives and committees.

For membership registration and member services, follow the *Member portal* link on the website or visit:
https://portal.nmaogun.com.ng

We encourage every colleague to visit the website, explore its pages and share the link within our professional community. Your participation, contributions and feedback will help keep it useful and relevant.

For website enquiries and contributions, please contact the Secretariat:
nmaogunstate@gmail.com

As Physicians’ Week continues, let us celebrate our fellowship, strengthen our professional bonds and renew our commitment to service.

*One profession. A shared purpose. A healthier Ogun.*

*General Secretary*
*Nigerian Medical Association, Ogun State Branch*
